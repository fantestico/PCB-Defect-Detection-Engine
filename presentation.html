The Challenge

  • Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs) frequently suffer from micron-level manufacturing anomalies.
  • Traditional manual inspection is slow, expensive, and prone to extreme human error.
  • Micro-defects like mouse bites, missing holes, and spurious copper lead to catastrophic hardware failure later in production.

Our Solution

An automated, end-to-end Computer Vision Dashboard designed specifically for brutal industrial environments.

Instantly scans incoming PCB photography to detect, localize, and classify anomalies with extreme bounding-box precision.

Under The Hood

The YOLOv8 Architecture

  • Trained on a comprehensive dataset of isolated PCB anomalies.
  • Generates rapid coordinate mapping & strict confidence metrics.
  • Capable of analyzing live imagery utilizing CPU or GPU fallback environments seamlessly.

Technology Stack

  • Core Vision: OpenCV Headless, Ultralytics YOLOv8
  • Data Processing: PyTorch, Pandas, Numpy Core
  • Deployment: Streamlit Community Cloud (Python 3.11)

Dashboard UI/UX

Built with a custom Titan Precision aesthetic:

  • Pure black backgrounds (`#000000`) for low eye-strain on the factory floor.
  • High-contrast NVIDIA-inspired Green (#76b900) telemetry alerts.
  • Dynamic, widescreen layout allowing side-by-side visualization and defect logging without scrolling.
